Not 4000 K (too warm — red ink reads as brown). Not 5600 K (too cool for aged paper). 5000 K is the D50 graphic-arts standard.

3. Saving Custom Mode C1 (on camera body, not Tether)

  1. Dial in all settings from the table above on the camera
  2. Menu → Setup (wrench) → Setting → Save to Custom Mode
  3. Select C1, confirm
  4. From now on, rotate the mode dial to C1 to restore everything instantly
Lumix Tether cannot save Custom Modes — this is camera-body only. Tether controls settings and triggers the shutter during shooting.

4. ColorChecker Passport Photo 2 — White & Gray Panels

White panel
(inside, brighter side)		 Sets custom white balance.
Place flat on easel, centered under both lights (same position as a calendar page). Do not move the camera — the panel does not need to fill the frame, only the center sampling rectangle.
Menu → Photo/Video → White Balance → select a custom slot (1/2/3/4) → Set → confirm panel is within the center crosshair → press shutter to sample. Camera locks neutral reference.
Tether: can switch to a saved custom WB slot but cannot perform the initial measurement — set once on camera body.
Gray panel
(inside, darker side — 18% gray)		 Verifies exposure.
Place flat on easel, centered (replacing white panel). Do not move the camera — the center-weighted meter reads off the gray panel as long as it is in the metering area. In aperture priority (f/5.6, ISO 200), check that the auto-selected shutter speed falls in the 1/60 – 1/125 s range. If too slow or too fast, adjust light brightness until it does.
Tether: meter readout visible in Tether — this check can be done entirely from the Mac.
Color patches (24 squares on reverse) — not used. The color patch grid builds a camera profile for color fidelity across sessions. This workflow cares about image clarity and detail for handwriting recognition, not color accuracy. Skip it.

5. Per-Session Sequence

1

Set up rig on floor marks. Close blinds. Power lights to 5000 K, equal output.

2

Turn mode dial to C1. Connect Lumix Tether. Confirm live view.

3

White panel flat on easel, centered → set custom WB on camera body (do not move camera; panel fills center sampling area from tripod position).

4

Gray panel flat on easel, centered → check auto shutter speed in live view (or Tether) is 1/60–1/125 s. Adjust light brightness if outside range.

5

Manual focus: zoom to 10× in live view on a page, adjust focus ring until ink is sharp. Set once for session.

6

Remove Passport. Load first page. Begin capture via Lumix Tether.

7

After last page: review frames before teardown. Reshoot anything soft or unevenly lit while the rig is still up.

8

Copy JPEGs from ~/Lumix Tether/ to madonnahist/test-data/samples/. Rename to YYYY-MM.jpg.

6. Setup Notes

  • Easel is 10″ in from the front edge of the table.
  • Camera tripod right rear leg: 44″ floor to hinge.
  • Light stands: 52″ vertical, floor to light attachment point.
  • Lights brightness at 14% yields 1/60 s exposure (at f/5.6, ISO 200).
