### iPhone PDF trap in the installed app (regression)
Gaylon reported that on iPhone a year PDF opened inside the home-screen app with no way to print it or get back, except by killing the app.
- **Cause:** Viewer V3 (98ea9e8) made the app installable (`display: standalone`, `scope: "/"`). Inside an installed iOS web app, a same-site link ignores `target="_blank"` and replaces the app's only window, which has no back button and no share controls. The July new-tab fix (td-863a4a) only helps in a Safari tab.
- **Fix:** `src/lib/viewer/pdf-share.svelte.ts`, used by the year page and the story page.
  - Only in the installed app: a PDF tap fetches the PDF in place and opens the iOS share sheet (Print, Save to Files, Books, Mail).
  - If iOS decides the tap expired during the download, a "Share or print the PDF" button asks for one more tap.
  - In a Safari tab nothing changes.
- Verified: check 0/0, viewer 90/90.
- Safari, real WebKit with installed-app mode and the share sheet faked in the page: 8/8.
  - In a normal tab the link still opens a new tab.
  - In the installed app, one tap shares the PDF (correct name and type, bytes start with `%PDF-`), the page never navigates, and no window opens.
  - A second tap reuses the fetched file.
  - An expired tap shows the "Share or print" button, which works.
- Still needs a real iPhone check after deploy: the share sheet itself can't be driven from a Mac.
