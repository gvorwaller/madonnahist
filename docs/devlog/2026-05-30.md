### Calibration Findings (classify + quality on 4 real samples, 1991–2022)
- **Corner sharpness is unusable as an absolute threshold** for documents —
  blank white cells score ~0–2 under the Laplacian, same as blur. Dropped from
  the verdict (still recorded). Clipped-pixel threshold raised to 20% (white
  paper sits near 255). All 4 samples now correctly `good`.
- **Classification switched from header-region regex to token geometry**: most
  prominent in-band **4-digit** year + a month accepted only if adjacent to it.
  Killed two failure modes — a tiny `DECEMBER 2021` thumbnail beating the real
  `2022` header, and stray tokens (`"may"`, handwritten `"22"`) read as
  month/year. Net: **zero confidently-wrong classifications**. Clean headers →
  `high`; year-only/ambiguous → correct year + `medium` "confirm manually";
  unrecognized handwriting headers → `low`/null for manual entry.
- **Grid layouts**: 5-row non-wrapped fits only a minority of months. Added
  `5-row-wrapped` (default) and `6-row-standard` (always fits). UI computes fit
  client-side and prevents picking a blocked layout.
