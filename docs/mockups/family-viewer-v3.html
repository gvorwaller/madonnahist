Family Viewer V3 — mockup

Vorwaller Family History

The new family viewer, screen by screen. Use the size toggle above to see every screen as a small phone, an iPhone, or a laptop: the layout follows the frame width, exactly as the real app will. Links inside the frames jump between screens. Each screen has a short What's new list and Questions for you.

Sample text only. Every diary entry, memory and name here is made up to show the layout. The real app shows only days Madonna has accepted, and only tags a person has confirmed.

Badges show the build order: Slice 1 is the first version the family sees; Slice 2–4 parts arrive in later updates.
